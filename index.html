about this placecat ~/faq.txt

about this place
who is naftali?

Naftali is my Hebrew name, the one people who actually know me use. Online I'm kestrel or 3hz. This site sits somewhere between public and private. The professional version of me is at mitchellberg.org.

why a separate site from 3hz.dev?

3hz.dev is the alias: security, linux, the technical stuff. This one is for everything that doesn't fit there, like writing and what I'm reading and thinking about. It's softer and less organized, and that's on purpose.

what does "naftaliro" mean?

Naftali is a Hebrew name that means "my struggle" or "one who wrestles." The "ro" is just what I added to get a handle nobody had taken yet. I like that the name is about effort. The wrestling is the point.
